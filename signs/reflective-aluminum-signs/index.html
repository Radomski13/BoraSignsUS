Frequently asked questions

What material should I choose for an outdoor sign?

Corrugated plastic is best for short-term use like elections, open houses and job sites. Aluminum lasts for years and suits permanent outdoor signs. Foam board and PVC are for indoor use.

Do yard signs come with stakes?

H-stakes are an add-on at checkout. Each stake fits one sign through the flutes.

Can you print both sides?

Yes. Choose double-sided during checkout. Both sides print the same or different artwork.

What file types do you accept?

PDF, AI, EPS, PNG and JPG. Vector files print sharpest. We send a free digital proof before anything prints.
