Frequently asked questions

How are banners finished?

Standard banners get a sewn or welded hem and brass grommets about every 2 feet. Pole pockets are available.

Mesh or vinyl?

Choose mesh for fences and windy areas, since air passes through. Choose vinyl for walls and storefronts.

What is the largest banner you print?

Up to 16 feet tall in one piece. Longer lengths are seamed or tiled.

Can I hang banners indoors?

Yes. Fabric banners are a good choice indoors because they photograph without glare.
