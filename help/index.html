Help center

Answers about ordering, artwork, proofs, shipping and our products.

Ordering and proofs

How does the free proof work?

After you order, we email a digital proof of your design. Approve it or ask for changes. We only start printing after you approve.

How long does production take?

Standard orders ship 2–3 business days after proof approval. Rush orders ship the next business day after approval.

What file types do you accept?

PDF, AI, EPS, SVG, PNG and JPG. Vector files print sharpest. If you send a low-resolution file, we will tell you on your proof.

Can you design my sign?

Yes. Add your logo, text and ideas in the production notes on any product and we will lay it out for your proof.

Signs

What material should I choose for an outdoor sign?

Corrugated plastic is best for short-term use like elections, open houses and job sites. Aluminum lasts for years and suits permanent outdoor signs. Foam board and PVC are for indoor use.

Do yard signs come with stakes?

H-stakes are an add-on at checkout. Each stake fits one sign through the flutes.

Can you print both sides?

Yes. Choose double-sided during checkout. Both sides print the same or different artwork.

What file types do you accept?

PDF, AI, EPS, PNG and JPG. Vector files print sharpest. We send a free digital proof before anything prints.

Banners

How are banners finished?

Standard banners get a sewn or welded hem and brass grommets about every 2 feet. Pole pockets are available.

Mesh or vinyl?

Choose mesh for fences and windy areas, since air passes through. Choose vinyl for walls and storefronts.

What is the largest banner you print?

Up to 16 feet tall in one piece. Longer lengths are seamed or tiled.

Can I hang banners indoors?

Yes. Fabric banners are a good choice indoors because they photograph without glare.

Flags & Fabric

What is included with a feather flag?

Printed flag, multi-piece pole and a ground spike. Cross bases and water bags are add-ons.

Single or double-sided?

Single-sided flags show a mirror image on the back. Double-sided flags read correctly on both sides with a blocker layer.

Can I wash my flag?

Yes. Machine wash cold on a gentle cycle and hang to dry.

How long do outdoor flags last?

Expect 3 to 6 months of daily outdoor use in strong sun and wind. Rotating flags extends their life.

Decals

Will window decals damage glass or paint?

No. Removable vinyl peels off cleanly within its rated lifespan. Permanent vinyl may need heat to remove.

Inside or outside mount?

Outside mount uses standard adhesive. Inside mount prints in reverse and sticks from inside the glass.

Is floor vinyl slippery?

Our floor laminate has a textured, slip-rated finish for commercial hard floors.

Do you install?

We offer local installation in the Tampa Bay area. Everywhere else, we ship ready to apply with instructions.

Magnets

How thick are your vehicle magnets?

Vehicle magnets are 30 mil, which holds well at highway speeds on flat steel panels.

Will a magnet damage my car paint?

Not when cared for. Remove and wipe both surfaces weekly so dirt and moisture do not get trapped.

Do magnets stick to aluminum?

No. Magnets only stick to steel. Many newer truck and van panels are aluminum, so test with a fridge magnet first.

Can I order one magnet?

Yes. Vehicle magnets have no minimum. Promo magnets start at 50 pieces.

Displays & Stands

Is the stand included?

Yes. Every display includes the hardware, printed graphic and carry bag or case.

Can I order just a replacement print?

Yes. Choose replacement graphic at checkout and use your existing stand.

How long does setup take?

Retractable stands take under 2 minutes. Pop-up walls take about 10 minutes with no tools.

Will it fit in my car?

Retractable stands pack to about 3 feet long. Pop-up cases roll on wheels and fit most SUV cargo areas.
