Frequently asked questions

What is included with a feather flag?

Printed flag, multi-piece pole and a ground spike. Cross bases and water bags are add-ons.

Single or double-sided?

Single-sided flags show a mirror image on the back. Double-sided flags read correctly on both sides with a blocker layer.

Can I wash my flag?

Yes. Machine wash cold on a gentle cycle and hang to dry.

How long do outdoor flags last?

Expect 3 to 6 months of daily outdoor use in strong sun and wind. Rotating flags extends their life.
