Frequently asked questions

How thick are your vehicle magnets?

Vehicle magnets are 30 mil, which holds well at highway speeds on flat steel panels.

Will a magnet damage my car paint?

Not when cared for. Remove and wipe both surfaces weekly so dirt and moisture do not get trapped.

Do magnets stick to aluminum?

No. Magnets only stick to steel. Many newer truck and van panels are aluminum, so test with a fridge magnet first.

Can I order one magnet?

Yes. Vehicle magnets have no minimum. Promo magnets start at 50 pieces.
