Frequently asked questions

Will window decals damage glass or paint?

No. Removable vinyl peels off cleanly within its rated lifespan. Permanent vinyl may need heat to remove.

Inside or outside mount?

Outside mount uses standard adhesive. Inside mount prints in reverse and sticks from inside the glass.

Is floor vinyl slippery?

Our floor laminate has a textured, slip-rated finish for commercial hard floors.

Do you install?

We offer local installation in the Tampa Bay area. Everywhere else, we ship ready to apply with instructions.
